## Storefront behavior

- Official current product-only images are referenced from Forge & Flame.
- Published prices are presented as manufacturer starting prices, not installed
  project totals. Delivery, venting, accessories, installation and tax are not
  included.
- Availability is not claimed. Every Quadra-Fire appliance requires contact with
  Aaron's Fireplace Co. to confirm current configuration, supply and delivery.
- Manufacturer specifications were transcribed from the official product pages.
  Heating capacity is a manufacturer guide and depends on the home and climate.
- No dealer-authorization status is claimed by the website.

## Removed appliance brands

All non-Lopi, non-Quadra-Fire complete pellet stoves and inserts were removed
from the appliance source files and image-upgrade index. Replacement parts are a
separate catalog and were not changed.
